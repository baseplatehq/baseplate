When a player touches a coin, play a coin sparkle and a pickup sound, then remove the coin.
I'll get a coin effect and a coin sound from Baseplate, then wire both into the coin touch handler.
baseplate · find_vfxquery: "coin pickup"
pickup/coin: Yellow coin pickup burst, about 0.6s, small. pickup/coin-medium: Yellow and grey coin pickup burst, about 1.1s, medium.
baseplate · find_sfxquery: "coin pickup"
pickup/coin-3: Coin pickup: 0.35s, tonal, high (about 1314 Hz), tail 0.19s.
baseplate · add_vfxnames: ["pickup/coin"]
Locked in vfx.json: pickup/coin. Pack unchanged since its scan.
baseplate · add_sfxnames: ["pickup/coin-3"]
Copied to assets/sounds/pickup/coin-3.ogg, uploaded to your Roblox account and registered in Sounds.luau.
Editsrc/client/Coins.client.luau
+ local Juice = require(ReplicatedStorage.Shared.Juice)
coin.Touched:Connect(function(hit)
if not Players:GetPlayerFromCharacter(hit.Parent) then return end
+ Juice.play("pickup/coin", coin.Position)
+ Juice.sfx("pickup/coin-3", coin.Position)
coin:Destroy()
Done. Touching a coin now plays pickup/coin and pickup/coin-3 where the coin was, then removes it. Press Play in Studio to try it.
